# Incident Communication

## Severity levels
Severity 1 means a full outage or data loss affecting customers. Severity 2 is major degradation of a core feature. Severity 3 is a minor issue with a workaround. Only Severity 1 and 2 incidents require a status page update.

## Update cadence
For Severity 1, post a status page update every 30 minutes until resolved. For Severity 2, post an update every 60 minutes. Each update must state the current impact, what is being done, and the time of the next update.

## Postmortems
A blameless postmortem is required for every Severity 1 incident and is due within 5 business days. The incident commander owns the document. Include a timeline, root cause, customer impact, and action items with named owners.
